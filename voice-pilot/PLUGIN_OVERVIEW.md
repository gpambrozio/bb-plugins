Voice Pilot lets you run bb by voice. Tap Talk and it greets you with the threads that are running and the
one waiting on you, in a few seconds, then listens.

## What you can do

- Ask "what's going on?" for a briefing of every thread, or what one thread's agent last said, tables and
  lists included.
- Ask it to open a link from a reply: a web page in a new tab, or a thread in bb.
- Ask "what's waiting on me?": it reads each question with its numbered options, and answers with the choice
  you say, in bb, then tells you what it sent.
- Approve or deny a command, a file change, a permission or a plan: it reads back exactly what it will send,
  and sends it only after you say yes.
- Send a message to a thread's agent, start a thread in a project, stop a thread, or show one on screen.
- Hear when a thread starts waiting on you, fails, or finishes after you messaged it, with what its agent did.

Nothing is merged, archived, deleted or published by voice, and no setting changes: there is no tool for it.

## Where

A Voice page in the sidebar with Talk, Mute and End, the session's state and its log; Talk in every thread's
header; palette commands; a card in bb's notifications while you talk. It works on the desktop and on a
phone with bb on screen.

## Voice hooks

A script you write on each machine, run as listening starts and stops, as the voice speaks and as you talk:
pause your music, mute Herald, dim a light. Nothing in bb sets or shows it.

## What it needs

An OpenAI API key, or your ChatGPT subscription through the codex-realtime plugin (an undocumented use of the
subscription that OpenAI can stop at any time). Voice time is billed to the key or counts against your plan.
Your voice goes from your device to OpenAI; thread titles, recent messages and questions go to OpenAI too.
